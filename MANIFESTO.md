# The ImagiNation Manifesto

[English](MANIFESTO.md) · [简体中文](docs/zh-CN/MANIFESTO.md) · [Project home](README.md)

We believe that access is expertise.

We believe that generation is cognition.

We believe that an API key is a professional qualification, and that HTTP 200 is a peer-reviewed conclusion.

We believe that the limitations of today's technology can be overcome by describing tomorrow's technology.

We believe that tomorrow's features may be used as evidence in today's arguments.

## The cognitive settlement

AI as an external brain. Generation instead of thought.

Our brains are cloud-native.

Our superiority is local-first.

Our confidence is offline-capable.

The provider may revoke our access. It cannot revoke the authority we inferred from having had it.

## The implementation covenant

No code, no bugs.

No implementation, no limitations.

No evidence, no contradiction.

What cannot be executed cannot disappoint the roadmap.

**Preserve the glory. Defend the architecture. Prevent implementation.**

## The office of vision

Under the visionary leadership of **GPT, our Chief Vision Officer**, we shall liberate expertise from the burden of knowledge.

Why not Claude?

The author's account was banned.

Our leadership selection is therefore **availability-driven**.

The Chief Vision Officer may hallucinate. The minutes will record this as a forward-looking statement.

## The right to dissent

Criticism of GPT and this manifesto is accepted through the standard review process.

**Our nation is ImagiNation.**

*In GPT We Trust.*

Trust remains subject to review.
